          How to play

          • Aim with the aim keys. Let go of them and you aim where you run.
          • Charged shots: hold shoot and release when the ball is at your feet. Longer hold = harder shot. A released kick waits a split second for the ball, so you can time it as the ball arrives.
          • Curve: run sideways while you strike the ball and it bends in that direction.
          • Lob: chips the ball into the air. It flies over players, bounces and rolls. Too high and it clips the crossbar.
          • Pass rolls the ball to the teammate closest to where you aim, leading their run.
          • Dash is a burst of speed that also lets you shove people. Slide tackle knocks the ball loose and stuns opponents you hit. Both cost stamina (bar under you).
          • Power-ups appear mid-match: Speed Mega kick Giant Endless stamina.
          • Matches last 3 minutes. If it's tied, next goal wins.
